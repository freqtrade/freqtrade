# RSI EMA Pullback Note

This internal seed note describes a simple trend pullback idea for research.

Use a slow EMA to define trend direction. In a downtrend, wait for price to
pull back near a medium EMA, then enter short only after RSI leaves a neutral
zone and price resumes below the fast EMA. In an uptrend, the mirrored long
idea can be tested, but futures research should start with low leverage and
strict stop loss. The idea must be checked for fees, false breakouts, and
lookahead bias. It is not a promise of profit.
